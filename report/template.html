Barnyard Monte Carlo

Every chicken, cow, sheep and pig simulated feed by feed and pet by pet, times per scenario, using the game's own rules from the Sunflower Land source. Profit is in FLOWER at today's P2P prices: produce sold minus the feed it ate.

(produce − feed − purchase + bounty) ÷ days owned, if sold the morning after it reaches that level. Peak = best level to sell, when a bounty for it exists.

Login timing decides how many pets you get

Each sleep has two love slots: the first opens one third into the sleep, the second one third after the first pet. Whether you catch both depends on when you log in, not how often.

Do cows, sheep and pigs get you the 9,000-ticket item?

The Duende Hat and Straw House cost 9,000 Duende Caps. Ticket bounties open on 9 Nov, so per-bounty ticket counts are not public yet; every assumption below is editable. Each bounty ticket also gives 5 chapter-pass points.
